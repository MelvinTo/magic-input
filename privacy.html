Minput Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Minput (also listed as MagicInput) is a voice keyboard for iPhone and iPad and a menu bar app for the Mac. You speak, a speech service turns what you said into text, and Minput types it where your cursor is. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short: Minput has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The microphone is used only while you dictate. Each dictation's audio, and a little text from around your cursor, goes to the speech service you chose, to be turned into text. Everything else stays on your device or in your own iCloud.

What goes to the speech service

When you dictate, Minput sends the speech service:

  • the recording of what you said (on iPhone, after the optional on-device filter that keeps only your voice);
  • your settings for that dictation, such as language and punctuation;
  • context that helps it get names and wording right: which app you are typing in (its identifier, and on the Mac its name), up to 1,500 characters before your cursor and 500 after it, and any text you have selected.

The service sends back the text, which Minput types for you. Two services can be chosen in Settings:

  • Dropline (the default), the speech-to-text service at dropline.fish.audio, reached through the developer's Dropline account. Dropline handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • minput-server, at the address set in Settings. Unless you change that address, it is a server run by the developer of Minput. It keeps what it worked out for each dictation (the recognized text and the steps to the final text) in memory for up to 48 hours, and on disk for 30 days for dictations made in comparison mode, so that recognition can be checked and improved. When it is in use, the words you add to the dictionary, and in comparison mode your ratings and corrections along with that dictation's recording, are sent to it too.

Neither service receives your name, email address, Apple ID or any identifier for you or your device, beyond the network address that every internet connection carries. Both are reached over encrypted connections.

What stays on your device

  • History: your recent dictations, their text, and their recordings, so you can copy one again or send a recording that didn't go through once more.
  • Your settings, your dictionary, and, if you set up "keep only my voice", your voiceprint: a short list of numbers that describes your voice, not a recording. The voiceprint is never uploaded.
  • A log of what the app did (steps, timings and errors; never the text you dictated). It leaves the device only if you share it yourself, from Settings → 导出诊断日志 (Export diagnostic log).
  • Recordings and results that "auto export", if you turn it on (it is off by default), saves into Minput's folder in the Files app.

The microphone and the keyboard

Minput turns the microphone on when you start a dictation and off when it ends. The keyboard needs Full Access so that it and the Minput app can pass recordings and results to each other on your device. The keyboard reads the text around your cursor only when you dictate, to send it as the context described above. It doesn't keep or send anything you type with other keyboards.

On the Mac, Input Monitoring is used only to notice the dictation hotkey (Fn + Control by default); your keystrokes are not recorded or sent anywhere. Accessibility is used to paste the text into the app in front.

iCloud

With iCloud sync on (the default; you can turn it off in Settings), Minput keeps your History, including its recordings, in your private iCloud database, and your settings in your iCloud key-value storage, so that your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID have them. Apple stores this under your Apple ID; the developer of Minput has no access to it.

Network checks

To tell you when the speech service may be unreachable, Minput checks whether it is up. When a dictation is slow, it also pings the nearest point of your own network, such as your Wi-Fi router or VPN gateway, to tell a network problem from a service problem. These checks carry no personal information.

Children

Minput is not directed at children.
