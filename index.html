Minput

A voice keyboard for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Speak, and clean text appears where your cursor is.

How it works

  • iPhone and iPad: switch to the Minput keyboard in any app. Tap the big key, speak, and tap it again; or hold it while you talk. The text is typed where your cursor is.
  • Mac: Minput lives in the menu bar. Hold Fn + Control, speak, and let go; the text is pasted into the app in front.
  • Filler words ("um", "呃") are removed, self-corrections are applied, and numbers are formatted. Chinese, English and a mix of both work.
  • History keeps your recent dictations, synced through iCloud, so you can copy one again or retry one that didn't go through.
